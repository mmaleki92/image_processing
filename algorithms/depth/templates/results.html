Depth Map Interpretation

The depth map shows the estimated distance of objects from the cameras:

  • Brighter colors (yellow to white): Objects closer to the cameras
  • Darker colors (blue to purple): Objects farther from the cameras
  • Black areas: Regions where depth couldn't be reliably estimated

For best results:

  • Ensure good lighting conditions
  • Use scenes with good texture and distinctive features
  • Keep cameras well calibrated and aligned
