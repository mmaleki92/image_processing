Instructions
  1. Start by clicking "Start Cameras" and allow camera access in your browser.
  2. For accurate depth, calibrate both cameras using a chessboard pattern (printed or on screen).
  3. After calibration, go to "View Results" to see the depth estimation.
  4. For optimal results, keep cameras parallel and about 6-10cm apart.
