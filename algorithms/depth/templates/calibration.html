Calibration Guide
  1. Print a chessboard pattern (9x6 inner corners) or display one on a screen
  2. Make sure both cameras can see the entire pattern
  3. Click "Capture Calibration Frame" to take a snapshot from both cameras
  4. Take at least 5 captures with the chessboard at different angles and positions
  5. Click "Start Calibration" to process the images
  6. Once calibration is complete, you can go back to view the results
